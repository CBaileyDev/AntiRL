## Position to take

AntiRL should be the private Windows coach that turns the player's saved match into one useful review, one short drill and one cue to test in the next session. A folder watcher detects files only after Rocket League saves them; it does not itself save every match or restore a lost BakkesMod autosave workflow. Explain that condition once during import setup.

Ship player-relative match results, reviewable moments and comparisons with the player's own recent same-mode matches. Add transparent pad routes, recovery time and heatmaps first. Kickoff roles/outcomes, 50/50 outcomes and touch quality need native-rate capture, clear possession/outcome definitions and annotated evaluation before their labels become recommendations. Geometry cannot establish intent or teammate blame.

## Cost discipline

This review made no real OpenAI/NeoToken calls and did not fetch paid-model pricing. Context probes measure character exposure, not billed tokens. A 47.8k-character context is roughly 12k tokens under the app's coarse four-characters-per-token assumption, before history, instructions, planner, retrieval, reasoning and output. That assumption is not an upper bound, especially for identifiers, JSON and non-Latin text.

Implementation should record provider-returned input/output/reasoning/cache usage, configured prices with source and effective date, cancellation behavior, and aggregate cost per accepted recommendation. With rates `input_price` and `output_price` per million tokens, base cost is `(input_tokens*input_price + output_tokens*output_price)/1e6`; include planner/retry/tool and provider-specific charges separately. Use a user-set maximum budget rather than guessed dollars. Cache the deterministic report and deduplicate identical replay/context explanations. A mockup cost chip is an illustrative interaction and must not masquerade as a measured quote.

## Research spec judgment

The repo research is a hypothesis backlog. Keep missing-versus-zero, identity/mode isolation, measured-versus-interpreted claims and avoidance of uncalibrated forecasts. Reject fixed universal boost targets, a hard 210-second eligibility gate and an automatic 20-match reassessment as launch laws. Weighted valid exposure and complete/forfeit context convey more than arbitrary duration exclusion. A “one focus” default should prioritize recommendations while retaining optional secondary observations. Honesty should be a claim contract, a confidence label and a short method popover, rather than repeated pessimistic paragraphs.
